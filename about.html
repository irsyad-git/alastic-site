About us

Small studio, focused tools.

Alastic is the brand of PT Alastic Global Industries, an independent software company based in Jakarta, Indonesia.

Our story

We started Alastic to make software that feels light: apps that open fast, do one job well and don't ask for more of your data than they need.

Our first products cover two very different needs — working with Word documents on the go, and keeping an eye on market prices without watching charts all day. What connects them is the same approach: practical, private and simple.

We're a small team, so every product gets close attention, and feedback from users goes straight to the people building it.

Anker: Commuter Story Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Anker: Commuter Story ("the app") is published on Google Play by Alastic Corp (PT Alastic Global Industries).

Data we collect

None. The app has no account, ads, analytics or trackers, and we receive no data from you.

Game progress

Progress, story choices and settings are stored only in your phone's local storage. Uninstalling the app also deletes everything.

Fonts from Google Fonts

The app loads fonts from Google Fonts. When it does, Google's service receives a standard technical request (for example your IP address), just as when you open any website. We send no other data and do not receive this data. See Google's privacy policy for details.

Permissions

Internet only, to load the fonts above.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

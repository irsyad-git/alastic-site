Prento — Privacy Policy

The app Prento: Thermal Receipt Print (id.alastic.prento) by PT Alastic Global Industries (Alastic Corp).

Summary

Prento works on your device. Receipts, sales, print history and settings are never uploaded. Data leaves your device only when you use Smart receipt or send feedback.

Data stored on your device

Store details you enter (name, address, phone, header and footer), receipts and their items, print history (including small previews of printed labels), sales figures and app settings. Everything is kept only in the app's local storage.

Bluetooth and local network

Prento uses Bluetooth to find and print to your thermal printer, and your local network only to print to a Wi‑Fi/LAN printer at the address you enter. Prento does not use Bluetooth or the network to track you or your location.

Camera, photos and files

Photos, PDFs and files you choose (for example shipping labels) are processed on your device to print them. They are not uploaded, except photos you attach to Smart receipt.

Smart receipt (AI)

If you use Smart receipt, the order text and any photos you attach are sent encrypted (HTTPS) to our server and to an AI provider only to turn them into receipt items. They are not stored after the response and are not used for advertising.

Feedback

If you use Send feedback, your message, topic, optional reply email and basic app info (app version, paper size, printer model, language) are sent encrypted to contact@alastic.id via the FormSubmit form service (formsubmit.co), only to reply and improve the app.

No tracking

No account, no ads, no analytics and no third‑party trackers.

Deleting data

Delete single prints in History, or use Settings > Delete all data. Deleting the app removes all its data.

Children

Prento is meant for a general audience and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes & contact

This policy may be updated at this address. Questions: contact@alastic.id. Effective 18 October 2026.

Prento — Kebijakan Privasi

Ringkasan

Prento bekerja di perangkat Anda. Struk, penjualan, riwayat cetak, dan pengaturan tidak pernah diunggah. Data hanya keluar dari perangkat saat Anda memakai Smart receipt atau mengirim masukan.

Data di perangkat

Detail toko yang Anda isi, struk dan itemnya, riwayat cetak (termasuk pratinjau kecil label), angka penjualan, dan pengaturan aplikasi. Semua hanya disimpan di penyimpanan lokal aplikasi.

Bluetooth dan jaringan lokal

Bluetooth dipakai untuk menemukan dan mencetak ke printer thermal Anda; jaringan lokal hanya untuk mencetak ke printer Wi‑Fi/LAN di alamat yang Anda isi. Tidak dipakai untuk melacak Anda atau lokasi Anda.

Kamera, foto, dan file

Foto, PDF, dan file yang Anda pilih (misalnya resi pengiriman) diproses di perangkat untuk dicetak dan tidak diunggah, kecuali foto yang Anda lampirkan ke Smart receipt.

Smart receipt (AI)

Jika Anda memakai Smart receipt, teks pesanan dan foto yang dilampirkan dikirim terenkripsi (HTTPS) ke server kami dan penyedia AI hanya untuk diubah menjadi item struk. Data tidak disimpan setelah diproses dan tidak dipakai untuk iklan.

Masukan

Jika Anda memakai Kirim masukan, pesan, topik, email balasan (opsional), dan info dasar aplikasi dikirim terenkripsi ke contact@alastic.id melalui FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), hanya untuk membalas dan memperbaiki aplikasi.

Tanpa pelacakan

Tanpa akun, iklan, analitik, atau pelacak pihak ketiga.

Menghapus data

Hapus cetakan satu per satu di Riwayat, atau Settings > Delete all data. Menghapus aplikasi juga menghapus seluruh datanya.

Perubahan & kontak

Kebijakan ini dapat diperbarui di alamat ini. Pertanyaan: contact@alastic.id. Berlaku sejak 18 Oktober 2026.

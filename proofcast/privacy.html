Proofcast — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Proofcast ("the app") is published by PT Alastic Global Industries ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Proofcast on Android or iOS.

Data stored on your device

Your starters, feeding logs, formulas, bake plans, journal entries, photos and settings are stored only on your device. We do not have access to them. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics or tracking SDK.

Feedback form (optional)

If you choose to use “Send feedback”, the message you write, the app version, language and platform — and your email address only if you enter one — are sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) through the FormSubmit service to our support inbox. We use it only to answer you and improve the app, do not sell or share it, and delete it on request.

Notifications

Step and starter reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No reminder data leaves your phone.

Backups and sharing

“Back up” creates a file that you save wherever you choose. “Share” uses your device's share sheet. We never receive these files.

Photos and camera

Photos you add to the journal are resized and stored inside the app on your device. Camera/photo access is requested only when you choose to add a photo.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Your data → Erase all data, or uninstall the app. To delete feedback you sent, email us.

Changes

We will update this page if this policy changes.

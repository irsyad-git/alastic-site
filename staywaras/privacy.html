StayWaras — Privacy Policy

Android app StayWaras (id.alastic.staywaras) by PT Alastic Global Industries (Alastic Corp). Last updated: 23 October 2026.

Summary

Your journal, mood check-ins, memories, streaks and chat history are stored on your phone. When you chat, the text of your recent messages (and the short "memories" you allowed the app to keep) is sent over HTTPS to our server, which passes it to an AI provider to write the reply. We do not store the content of your messages on our servers. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

What leaves your phone

Chat requests: your latest messages (about the 16 most recent turns), the character you chose, your language, an "under 18" flag, your short memories, and a random install ID. The install ID is used only for rate limiting and the free daily limit, is not linked to your name, and is kept temporarily in server memory.

AI provider: Anthropic processes each request to generate the reply, under its commercial API terms. Under those terms, API content is not used to train its models by default, and Anthropic may keep it for a limited period for safety and abuse prevention. See anthropic.com/legal/privacy.

Feedback and AI-reply reports: if you send them, the message, topic, optional name/email, app version and language are sent through FormSubmit to our support inbox. A report contains only the single AI reply you report.

What never leaves your phone

Birth year, journal entries, mood check-ins, streaks, points, badges and the full chat history.

Your controls

In the app you can see, edit and delete what your character remembers, export your data, delete chat history, or delete everything (Delete account and all data). Uninstalling removes everything on the phone. Android cloud backup is disabled for this app. To delete feedback you sent, email contact@alastic.id.

Safety

The app checks your messages on the device for signs of crisis and, if found, shows helplines instead of AI-written replies. Helpline numbers come from public sources and may change; they are not affiliated with us.

AI notice

The characters are AI. They can make mistakes and are not a medical, therapy or counselling service. If you are in danger, contact local emergency services.

Age

For ages 13 and over. Users aged 13–15 need a parent or guardian's agreement. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Notifications

Optional daily reminders are scheduled on your phone; no data is sent for this.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: contact@alastic.id · https://alastic.id

StayWaras — Kebijakan Privasi

Ringkasan

Jurnal, cek mood, ingatan, streak, dan riwayat chat kamu disimpan di HP kamu. Saat kamu chat, teks pesan terbaru (dan "ingatan" singkat yang kamu izinkan disimpan) dikirim lewat HTTPS ke server kami, lalu diteruskan ke penyedia AI untuk menulis balasan. Kami tidak menyimpan isi pesanmu di server. Tidak ada akun, iklan, atau analitik.

Yang keluar dari HP

Permintaan chat: pesan terbaru (sekitar 16 giliran terakhir), karakter pilihan, bahasa, penanda "di bawah 18", ingatan singkat, dan ID instalasi acak. ID ini hanya untuk pembatasan laju dan batas harian gratis, tidak terhubung ke namamu, dan disimpan sementara di memori server.

Penyedia AI: Anthropic memproses tiap permintaan untuk membuat balasan, sesuai ketentuan komersial API-nya. Menurut ketentuan itu, konten API tidak dipakai melatih model secara default, dan Anthropic dapat menyimpannya untuk jangka terbatas demi keamanan dan pencegahan penyalahgunaan. Lihat anthropic.com/legal/privacy.

Masukan dan laporan balasan AI: jika kamu kirim, pesan, topik, nama/email opsional, versi aplikasi, dan bahasa dikirim lewat FormSubmit ke kotak masuk dukungan kami.

Yang tidak pernah keluar dari HP

Tahun lahir, isi jurnal, cek mood, streak, poin, lencana, dan riwayat chat lengkap.

Kendalimu

Di aplikasi kamu bisa melihat, mengubah, dan menghapus ingatan karakter, mengekspor data, menghapus riwayat chat, atau menghapus semuanya. Mencopot aplikasi menghapus semua data di HP. Cadangan cloud Android dinonaktifkan. Untuk menghapus masukan yang pernah dikirim, email contact@alastic.id.

Keselamatan

Aplikasi memeriksa pesanmu di perangkat untuk tanda krisis dan, bila ada, menampilkan layanan bantuan alih-alih balasan AI. Nomor layanan berasal dari sumber publik dan dapat berubah.

Pemberitahuan AI

Karakter adalah AI. Mereka bisa keliru dan bukan layanan medis, terapi, atau konseling. Jika kamu dalam bahaya, hubungi layanan darurat setempat.

Usia

Untuk usia 13 tahun ke atas; usia 13–15 memerlukan persetujuan orang tua atau wali.

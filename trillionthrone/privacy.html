Trillion Throne Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Trillion Throne ("the app") is published on Google Play by Alastic Corp (PT Alastic Global Industries).

Data we collect

None. The app has no account, ads, analytics or trackers, and we receive no data from you.

Game progress

Your save, settings and best scores are stored only in your phone's local storage. Uninstalling the app also deletes everything.

Permissions

None. The app works fully offline and does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, location or files.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
